## Limits

Post-level labels and text-only features on one dataset; the test split has 450 rows. About 14 rows is the rough floor for an uncorrected McNemar test; after Holm correction over the family the floor is higher, and only the Holm column decides. Attributions describe the model, not the language, and are not causal claims.
