## RQ2 support. Are the explanations faithful to the model?

Sources: `results_summary/11_MULTI-CHECKPOINT_LOGIT-POOL/faithfulness_test.json`, `results_summary/rq/rq2_faithfulness.csv`.

Deletion test on 200 of the 200 test posts that every comparison technique explained (this technique explained 200 in all). For each k, the k words the post's own explanation ranks highest toward EXTREMIST are deleted, against deleting as many random words of the same post (5 seeds). ## Limits

Post-level labels and text-only features on one dataset; the test split has 450 rows. About 14 rows is the rough floor for an uncorrected McNemar test; after Holm correction over the family the floor is higher, and only the Holm column decides. Attributions describe the model, not the language, and are not causal claims.
